07GOOD QUESTIONS

Clear answers
build trust.

How Gaia works, what the data means and where people stay in control.

Are these real products, and will my order ship? +

The store is a demo catalog with sample products and prices. UPI and cash-on-delivery buttons confirm a local demonstration only; no seller receives an order and nothing is shipped.

Does the UPI option collect money? +

No. The UPI demo confirms the sample order immediately without opening a payment app or contacting a bank.

How does Gaia AI help? +

Gaia can explain environmental topics, connect signals to possible risks and suggest places to start. People bring local knowledge and make the decisions.

Is Gaia using live environmental data? +

The weather panel can request a current forecast for your location through Open-Meteo, only after you choose to share your location. Other scenarios on this page are clearly labeled as illustrative.

Can one weather reading explain climate change? +

No. Weather describes short-term conditions. Climate is understood through long-term patterns across many observations.

What happens when live data is unavailable? +

The weather panel explains that the reading could not be reached and shows an example scenario labeled as demo data. Gaia AI also has a local environmental guide for common questions.

Can students use Gaia? +

Yes. Gaia is designed to make environmental ideas easier for students, teachers and curious visitors to explore together.
